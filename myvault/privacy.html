Folkfile

Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

The short version
  • Folkfile doesn't collect any data. Your documents and details stay on your iPhone.
  • There's no account, no server, no analytics and no ads.
  • Reading documents and answering questions happen on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device technology.

Who we are

Folkfile is made by CJ Catalan ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the Folkfile app for iPhone handles your information.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal information. Folkfile has no account to create, no server that holds your data, and no analytics, advertising or tracking. The app doesn't connect to the internet to send your information anywhere.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you add to Folkfile stays on your device:

  • Scans, photos and files you add, and the details read from them
  • Details you type in yourself, including profiles for you and your family

What you type in the search box and the answers shown aren't saved.

This information is encrypted on your iPhone, and the encryption key is kept in your iPhone's Keychain, set so it can't leave this device. Folkfile's data is excluded from iCloud and computer backups, so it isn't copied to Apple's servers or anywhere else.

How Folkfile reads your documents

Folkfile reads text in your documents using Apple's on-device text recognition. It uses Apple's on-device language model to label details and understand your questions. This all happens on your iPhone. Folkfile doesn't use any cloud AI service, including Apple's cloud models.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you choose to scan a document.
  • Photos and Files: Folkfile uses Apple's pickers, so it only receives the photos or files you choose. It can't see the rest of your photo library or files.
  • Face ID or passcode: used to unlock the app. Face ID is handled by iOS; Folkfile never receives your face data.
  • Notifications: used for reminders, such as before a document expires. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They're worded so they don't reveal your information, for example "A document expires in 2 months." They never include a document's name, its owner or numbers like a passport or account number.

Sharing documents to Folkfile

When you share a file to Folkfile from another app, such as Mail or Safari, the file is kept briefly in a protected area on your iPhone until you review it in the app. It's deleted from there once you save or cancel.

Sharing documents from Folkfile

You can choose to share a document, for example as a PDF or as text. Folkfile only shares when you tap Share, and the iPhone's share sheet sends it to the app or person you pick. Once shared, a copy is outside Folkfile's protection, so share only with people you trust.

Backups

Because Folkfile's data isn't included in iCloud or computer backups, Folkfile lets you make your own backup file. It's encrypted with a password you choose, and you decide where to save it, for example in Files, iCloud Drive or on a computer. Folkfile never uploads it. Without your password or recovery key, nobody can open it, including us. If you lose both, the backup can't be restored.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Crash reports

Folkfile doesn't include any crash reporting or analytics tools. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. These never include your documents or details.

Deleting your data

You can delete documents, details and people in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. Because Folkfile keeps nothing on a server, deleted data can't be recovered by us. You can restore it only from a backup file you made.

Children

Folkfile isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects how your information is handled, we'll also explain it in the app before it takes effect.
